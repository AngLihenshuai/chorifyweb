Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026

What we collect

Chorify processes webcam frames and audio locally on your device to detect pose landmarks and the music envelope. None of this video or audio leaves your machine.

OpenAI API

If you provide an OpenAI API key, optional natural-language feedback is sent to OpenAI under their terms. You may use Chorify without supplying a key.

Bluetooth

Chorify communicates locally with your paired wearable using the Nordic UART BLE service. Pairing data is stored only on your device.

Contact

This is an open-source project. Issues and questions are welcome on the GitHub repository.
