Features

Everything you need to learn a routine.

Chorify combines computer vision, audio analysis, and embedded hardware into one fluid practice loop.

AI Pose Detection

MediaPipe extracts 19 keypoints from your webcam and the reference clip in real time, then compares angle and offset frame-by-frame.

Audio Visualization

Live waveform plus a 10ms-resolution amplitude envelope drive both the visualizer and the haptic intensity.

Bluetooth Haptics

Pairs with any Nordic UART-compatible board (Adafruit, ESP32). Vibration scales with music intensity and triggers on missed moves.

Smart Playback

Toggle 0.5× / 1.0× speed, scrub the timeline, and start with a 5-second countdown so you can warm up before the beat drops.

Performance Tracking

Logs pauses, seeks, slow-mo usage, and per-frame accuracy. Review what you struggled with and where you nailed it.

Visual Feedback

Your camera frame glows red when you're off the choreography and stays clean when you're in sync — instant cue, zero menu-diving.

Workflow

From upload to muscle memory.

A single Python app stitches video, vision, and hardware into one continuous feedback loop.

01

Load a video

Drop in any MP4, AVI, or MOV. Chorify decodes the audio track and extracts a reference pose stream.

02

Pair the device

Toggle Bluetooth and connect your ESP32 wearable. codepy_backup.py ships ready to flash.

03

Hit play

A 5-second countdown gives you time to set up. Then your webcam pose is compared frame-by-frame against the reference.

04

Feel the beat

The music envelope drives haptic vibration; mismatches push a sharper pulse so your body learns without looking.

Get started

Run Chorify in 4 steps.

  1. Clone or download the project source.
  2. Create a .env file with OPENAI_API_KEY="your-key".
  3. Activate a virtualenv and install: pip install -r requirements.txt.
  4. Run: python main.py — you should see the Chorify window.

For haptic feedback, flash codepy_backup.py to your ESP32 board, then pair from inside the app.
